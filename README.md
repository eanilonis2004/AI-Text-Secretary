# AI Text-to-Calendar Secretary

A personal, single-user backend that turns plain-language text messages into confirmed Google Calendar events — no manual entry required.

Someone texts you *"dinner tomorrow at 7?"* or just states *"we're grabbing lunch at 12"* — this service parses it with an LLM, sends you a confirmation notification, and adds it straight to your Google Calendar the moment you say yes. Miss the notification? It's not gone — every parsed invite is saved as **pending** until you act on it, so you can check back and confirm hours or days later.
